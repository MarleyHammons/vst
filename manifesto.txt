THIS INTERFACE MUST FEEL LIKE A SIGNAL BREAKING APART.

IT SHOULD NOT LOOK CLEAN, SAFE, OR PERFECT. IT SHOULD FEEL UNSTABLE, LIKE SOUND BEING STRETCHED, COPIED, DAMAGED, AND SENT BACK THROUGH ITSELF. EVERY CONTROL SHOULD FEEL CONNECTED TO THE DELAY EFFECT, AS IF TURNING A KNOB IS ALSO BENDING THE SIGNAL.

THE COLORS SHOULD BE DARK AND HEAVY, WITH SHARP NEON HIGHLIGHTS THAT FEEL LIKE DIGITAL NOISE. THE TEXT SHOULD LOOK DIRECT AND MECHANICAL, BUT THE LAYOUT SHOULD FEEL SLIGHTLY OFF BALANCE. THE USER SHOULD FEEL LIKE THEY ARE WORKING INSIDE A BROKEN MACHINE THAT IS STILL ALIVE.

THE PLUG-IN SHOULD NOT HIDE ITS DISTORTION. IT SHOULD SHOW INTERFERENCE, DECAY, AND FEEDBACK AS PART OF ITS IDENTITY.
